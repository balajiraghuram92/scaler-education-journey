Lesson 1 · Foundations

#### Build & ship your first AI app

- What an LLM is: tokens, next-token prediction, why it has no memory
- Your first 6 lines against the OpenAI API, and the 3 chat roles
- Swapping to a free model on Groq with one `base_url`
- Gradio: a web app in 4 lines

**Build** AI Website Summarizer, then post it on LinkedIn

Lesson 2 · Foundations

#### LangChain & your first agent

- Three ways to make a model do what you want

- Tools and the think → call tool → answer loop

Lessons 3–4 · Knowledge

#### RAG: give your AI its own library

- Embeddings and cosine similarity, drawn in 2D first

- Where it goes: agentic and advanced RAG

**Build** Chat with your PDF, with citations

Lesson 5 · Ship it

#### Dockerize everything

#### AI agents on AWS
